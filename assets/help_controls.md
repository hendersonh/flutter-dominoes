# Controls

- Tap a tile to play it (if there's only one valid side it is placed automatically). If tile can be played at both ends, select green marker at desired end to be played.  
- AI Brain: Adjust the smartness of AI players by tapping the Settings icon on the menu.
- To change the scoring system, which restarts the game, tap the circular icon. 
- To give feedback, send email to hendy@hendersonhood.com with the phrase "game feedback" as the subject.
