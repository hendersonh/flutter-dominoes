# Game Rules

- Objective: Be the first player to play all your tiles or have the lowest score if the game is blocked.
- First Move: The player with the [6/6] tile starts. In subsequent rounds, the winner of the previous round starts.
- Matching: You can only play a tile if one of its ends matches one of the open ends on the board.
- Passing: If you cannot play, you must pass your turn.
- Blocking: If no player can move, the round ends with a "Block."

STATUS INDICATORS:
- The game mode (First to Target or Six-love) and AI difficulty (Rockie, Casual, Pro or Legend) or displayed center top of the board.
