Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 16, 2026

No Personal Data Collection

Brut Dominoes (HendyDominoes) is designed to respect your privacy. The application **does not collect, transmit, or share** any personal information, location data, or contact lists.

Local Storage Only

All game settings, match history, and performance metrics (Ranks A/B/C/D) are stored **locally on your device**. This data never leaves your phone and is not synchronized to any cloud servers.

No Third-Party Tracking

We do not use any third-party analytics (like Google Analytics or Firebase), advertising identifiers, or tracking cookies inside the application.

Permissions

The app may require standard permissions for high-fidelity audio output and haptic feedback. These permissions are used exclusively for gameplay immersion and are not used to access sensitive data.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the official GitHub repository or support channels.
