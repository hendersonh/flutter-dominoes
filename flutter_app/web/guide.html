Partners & Cut-Throat Dominoes UI Guide

🧭 Navigating the Interface

Welcome to HendyDom. The game's interface is designed to be sleek, intuitive, and responsive.

1. Match Setup Menu

Before any domino is played, you'll be greeted by the Match Setup screen. Here you will define the rules of engagement:

  • Play Style: Select either CUT-THROAT (1 vs 1 vs 1 vs 1) for a solo challenge, or PARTNERS (2 vs 2) to team up with an AI ally against two opponents.
  • Game Mode & Target Goals: Choose your arena. You can select a traditional point-based target match (with configurable finish lines of 100, 150, or 200 points) or dive into the historic Jamaican Six-Love matching rules.
  • AI Difficulty: Select your opponent's skill level—choose from Rookie, Casual, Pro, or Legend. In Partners mode, your teammate will operate at the same skill level as your opponents.

2. The Match HUD (Heads-Up Display)

During a game, keep your eyes on the HUD overlay. It provides realtime match telemetry:

  • Scores & Standings: View current round points and aggregate match wins. In Partners mode, your score is combined with your teammate's score for a shared total.
  • The Champion Crown (👑): Pay close attention to the leaderboards. The all-time match leader will wear the digital crown directly beside their name on the HUD.
  • Hand & Score Counters (#/#): Just beneath each player's name, you will see a pair of numbers separated by a slash. The first number represents the total number of dominoes currently in that player's hand. The second number indicates that player's (or team's) accumulated score in the current match.

3. Making Your Move

Interaction is optimized purely for touch devices. Simply tap any playable domino in your hand. If the move is valid, the game will automatically place it onto the active board for you. Invalid dominoes will remain securely in your hand.

🎯 The Scoring System

HendyDom features two dramatically different competitive arenas with their own distinct scoring philosophies.

Point-Based Target Score Mode

The global standard. Over multiple rounds, players accumulate the pip values of dominoes left in their opponents' hands when the round ends. The first player (or team) to reach or exceed your chosen target score limit (100, 150, or 200 points) wins the match.

"Six-Love" Mode

The authentic Jamaican gauntlet. Points don't matter across rounds; only pure round victories counts. The first player (or team) to secure 6 separate round wins instantly wins the championship.

What is a "Game Bruk"?
In Six-Love mode, if the competition is so fierce that EVERY side managed to win at least one round, a "Game Bruk" triggers.

• In Cut-Throat: Triggered when all 4 players have at least 1 round win.
• In Partners: Triggered when both teams have at least 1 round win.

All scores are instantly reset to zero, and the match restarts from the beginning!

🏆 End of Match Reporting

When the dust settles and a champion is crowned, the application instantly transitions to the post-match debrief.

The Summary Modal

A cinematic Game Over screen will overlay the board. It features:

  • The Champion Reveal: The victor is highlighted, displaying their legendary crown (👑) status if applicable.
  • Lifetime Metrics & Win Distribution: A complete breakdown of all historical match results, helping you track your long-term success against the AI.

The Performance Ranking System (A/B/C/D)

At the end of a match, your performance is graded with a letter rank that perfectly mirrors the AI difficulty level you chose to face. This proves the exact caliber of opponents you managed to defeat (or lose to):

  • Rank A (Legend Difficulty): The ultimate prestige. Earning this means you have been sitting at the table playing against 3 uncompromising Dominoes Masters. Only the elite survive here.
  • Rank B (Pro Difficulty): A highly respected competitive rank. You are playing against seasoned, strategic opponents who rarely make mistakes.
  • Rank C (Casual Difficulty): The standard ranking. You faced average opponents playing predictably but competently.
  • Rank D (Rookie Difficulty): The training wheels rank. You played against beginners who often make random or suboptimal moves.

Reading Your Lifetime Metrics:
Your cumulative history is displayed as a series of rank counts. For example, A*3 B*2 C*4 D*3 means you have completed 3 matches at the Legend level, 2 at Pro, 4 at Casual, and 3 at the Rookie level.

Next Actions

You have total control over the flow of your session. At the bottom of the reporting modal, choose your path:

  • PLAY AGAIN: Bypasses the setup menu entirely. Instantly drops you into a fresh round retaining your exact difficulty and mode settings. Ideal for non-stop action.
  • CHANGE RULES: Returns you to the main Match Setup menu if you want to dial up the AI difficulty or switch scoring formats.
